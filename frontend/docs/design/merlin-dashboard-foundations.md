# Merlin Dashboard Foundations

## Calm Futuristic OS
- Prioritize spacious, luminous canvases with glassy depth and subtle grain over cluttered blocks.
- Keep typography restrained: short, precise phrases instead of slogans or verbose labels.
- Let every interaction feel purposeful—nothing moves without intent, nothing shouts.

## Reduction Rules
- Remove redundant headings; preserve only the signal the user needs right now.
- Avoid multi-word labels unless they add clarity; favor single nouns or verbs.
- Limit UI chatter: tooltips, badges, and helper text appear only when the context demands them.

## Visual Hierarchy
- Anchor the experience with a top-center hero presence (the Orb) while supporting elements stay grounded.
- Give priority to light, centered cards with generous negative space around them.
- Maintain a calm visual rhythm: consistent gutters, gentle gradients, and restrained glow.

## Spacing Rhythm
- Lay out content within a vertical rhythm of approximately 1.5rem (24px) increments.
- Use horizontal gutters that flex with `clamp` to keep edges breathing on any viewport.
- Allow components to span across space without forcing literal separators—spacing is the divider.

## Holo Engine Level 1

### Materials
- Glass + mesh light surfaces with soft bloom, subtle grain overlays, and minimal borders keep focus on content.

### Motion
- Movements are slow and deliberate; avoid arcade or overly kinetic animation that would break the calm.

### Orb
- The Orb is a state instrument, not an avatar. It sits as a hero presence at the top-center of the workspace.
- It reacts to system status but never disappears into a mode toggle—this ensures consistency across views.

### Command Line
- Reserve the bottom-center for a single primary intent surface whose visual placeholder communicates readiness.
- The surface is fixed; future interaction layers will grow out of this anchor.

### Dock
- Keep the dock persistent, low-noise, and tactile but restrained: it supports navigation without demanding attention.
