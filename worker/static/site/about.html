About Fish Delish

Fish Delish was founded on the Adriatic coast of Croatia. Our cuisine celebrates the region's fresh seafood and time-honoured recipes. We keep things simple: seasonal catches, fresh herbs, good olive oil, and recipes passed down through family kitchens.

What we serve

Our menu focuses on classic Adriatic dishes — grilled whole fish, seafood platters, light salads, and small plates that highlight fresh, local ingredients.

Our story

Rooted in Croatian coastal food traditions, Fish Delish brings coastal flavours and a relaxed dining experience. We are bringing this offering to Portugal and look forward to sharing our food with the local community.
